# Academic Integrity Policy

## Plagiarism checks

All theses and project reports are screened using plagiarism-detection software. The similarity index must be below 15 percent, excluding the list of references, for the report to be accepted for evaluation.

## Penalties for misconduct

For a first offence, the student receives zero marks in the assessment component and a written warning. A second offence results in an F grade in the course. Serious cases, such as impersonation in an examination, are referred to the Disciplinary Committee, which may impose suspension for up to one academic year or expulsion.

## Use of generative AI tools

Students may use generative AI tools only when the course instructor has explicitly permitted it. When such tools are used, the student must disclose the tool and the prompts in an appendix. Undisclosed use of AI tools is treated as academic misconduct.

## Group work and collaboration

Collaboration is allowed only on assignments that are labelled as group work. In individual assignments, sharing code, solutions, or written text with other students is not permitted, even if the content is later modified.

## Appeals

A student may appeal a penalty to the Academic Appeals Committee within 10 working days of receiving the decision. The committee gives its decision within 21 days of receiving the appeal.
