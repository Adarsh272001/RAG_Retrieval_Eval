# Attendance Policy

This policy applies to all students of the Northfield Institute of Technology (a fictional institute used as a sample corpus for this project).

## Minimum attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in each course to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated separately for lectures and laboratory sessions, and a student must meet the requirement in both.

## Condonation of shortage

The Dean of Academics may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 10 percent, meaning attendance between 65 and 74 percent, on medical or family-emergency grounds. A condonation fee of Rs 500 per course applies. Medical certificates must be submitted within 7 days of resuming classes. Applications submitted after that period are not considered.

## Attendance below 65 percent

Students with attendance below 65 percent in a course are awarded an I (Incomplete) grade and must repeat the course in its next offering. No condonation is available in this case.

## Official duty leave

Students representing the Institute in approved events receive on-duty leave. Such days count as present, up to a maximum of 15 days per semester. The faculty coordinator must forward the approved list to the department office at least 3 working days before the event.

## Recording of attendance

Attendance is recorded through the biometric terminal at the classroom entrance within the first 10 minutes of the session. Students arriving after 10 minutes are marked absent for that session, but they may still attend the class.
