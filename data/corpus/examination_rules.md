# Examination Rules

## Assessment structure

Each course has continuous internal assessment worth 40 marks and an end-semester examination worth 60 marks. The internal component consists of two mid-semester tests of 15 marks each and assignments worth 10 marks.

## Conduct in the examination hall

Students must carry their institute ID card to every examination. Entry to the hall is permitted up to 30 minutes after the examination starts, and no student may leave during the first 45 minutes. Mobile phones and smartwatches are prohibited in the examination hall. Any such device found with a student is confiscated and reported to the Controller of Examinations.

## Calculators

Only non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted. Calculators with memory storage or graphing functions are not allowed, and the invigilator may inspect any calculator before the paper begins.

## Re-evaluation of answer scripts

A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within 5 working days of the declaration of results. The fee is Rs 1,000 per course. The outcome of re-evaluation is final, and marks may increase, remain the same, or decrease.

## Supplementary examinations

Students who fail in up to 2 courses in a semester may appear in the supplementary examination, held within 6 weeks of the declaration of results. The grade obtained in a supplementary examination is capped at B.

## Missed examinations

A student who misses an end-semester examination for a valid medical reason may request a make-up examination. The request requires a medical certificate and the approval of the Dean of Academics.
