# Fees and Refunds

## Payment of tuition fee

The tuition fee is due within 15 days of the start of the semester. A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged, up to a maximum of Rs 3,000. If the fee remains unpaid beyond 45 days, the student's name is struck off the rolls.

## Refund on withdrawal

A student who withdraws before the commencement of classes receives a refund of 90 percent of the tuition fee. Withdrawal within the first 15 days of classes earns a refund of 50 percent. No refund is given for withdrawal after 15 days.

## Caution deposit

A caution deposit of Rs 10,000 is collected at admission. It is refundable within 30 days of graduation, after the student obtains a no-dues clearance from the library, hostel, and laboratories.

## Instalments

Students may pay the tuition fee in two instalments with prior approval from the Accounts office. A processing fee of Rs 750 applies to the instalment facility.

## Modes of payment

Fees can be paid online through the student portal or by demand draft. Cash is not accepted at the Accounts counter.

## Sibling concession

A tuition waiver of 10 percent is given to the second sibling when two siblings are studying at the Institute at the same time.
