# Grading and Promotion

## Grade scale

Marks are converted to letter grades as follows: O (10 points) for 90 and above, A+ (9 points) for 80 to 89, A (8 points) for 70 to 79, B+ (7 points) for 60 to 69, B (6 points) for 50 to 59, C (5 points) for 45 to 49, and F (0 points) for below 45. To pass a course a student must score at least 45 percent overall and at least 40 percent in the end-semester examination.

## SGPA and CGPA

The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the sum of credits multiplied by grade points for the semester, divided by the total credits of the semester. The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated the same way across all completed semesters.

## Promotion to the third semester

To move to the third semester, a student must have cleared at least 60 percent of the credits registered in the first two semesters. A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to continue in the programme.

## Academic probation

A student whose CGPA remains below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters is placed on academic probation. During probation the student must meet the academic advisor every month and may register for a reduced course load.

## Award of the degree

The MBA degree is awarded after the successful completion of 96 credits with a minimum CGPA of 5.5.

## Grade improvement

A student may reappear to improve the grade in up to 2 courses. The improvement attempt must be made within one year after the final semester.
