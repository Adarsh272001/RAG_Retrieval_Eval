# Hostel Rules

## Gate timings

Hostel gates close at 10:30 PM for first-year students and at 11:30 PM for all other residents. Entering after closing time requires prior written permission from the warden.

## Visitors

Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' lounge, between 4 PM and 7 PM on weekends. Visitors are not permitted inside rooms, and overnight guests are not allowed under any circumstances.

## Room allocation and change

Rooms for senior students are allotted on the basis of CGPA, while first-year students are allotted rooms by lottery. Requests to change rooms are accepted only during the first 2 weeks of a semester.

## Prohibited appliances

Electric heaters, immersion rods, and induction cooktops are prohibited in hostel rooms. A violation attracts a fine of Rs 2,000 and confiscation of the appliance.

## Mess charges and rebate

Mess charges are Rs 4,200 per month. A rebate of Rs 120 per day is available if a resident is absent for more than 5 consecutive days and has informed the mess committee in advance.

## Damage to property

Residents are collectively responsible for damage in common areas. The cost of repairs is divided equally among the residents of the floor unless the person responsible is identified.
