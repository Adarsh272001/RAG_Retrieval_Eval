# Internships and Placements

## Mandatory summer internship

Every student must complete an 8-week summer internship after the second semester. The internship carries 4 credits. The internship report, along with a certificate from the industry mentor, must be submitted within 2 weeks of completing the internship.

## Placement eligibility

To register for campus placements, a student must have a CGPA of at least 6.0 and no active backlogs.

## One-offer policy

A student who accepts a job offer through campus placement may sit for another company only if the new offer has a package at least 1.5 times the first offer.

## Withdrawal from an offer

A student who withdraws after accepting an offer is debarred from the rest of the placement process, and the placement cell may inform the recruiting company about the reason.

## Students opting for higher studies

Students who plan to pursue higher studies must deregister from the placement process by the end of the third semester. They may re-register only with the permission of the Placement Officer.

## Dress code and conduct during recruitment

Students must wear formal attire during pre-placement talks and interviews. Being late for a recruiter's session without prior intimation can lead to suspension of placement privileges for that company.
