# Library Rules

## Borrowing limits

Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6 books at a time for a period of 21 days. Faculty members may borrow up to 10 books for 60 days. Reference books and journals cannot be taken out of the library.

## Renewals

A book can be renewed up to 2 times, provided that no other member has reserved it. Renewal can be done online through the library portal before the due date.

## Fines and lost books

An overdue fine of Rs 2 per day is charged for each book. If a book is lost, the borrower must pay the replacement cost of the book along with a processing fee of Rs 200.

## Opening hours

The library is open from 8 AM to 10 PM on weekdays and from 9 AM to 5 PM on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays. During the examination period the closing time is extended to midnight.

## Digital resources

Electronic journals and databases are accessible through the campus VPN. Students who want to use these resources off campus must obtain VPN credentials from the IT helpdesk.

## Behaviour in the library

Silence must be maintained in the reading halls. Food and beverages, other than water in a closed bottle, are not allowed.
