# Scholarships and Fee Waivers

## Merit scholarship

Students with a CGPA of 9.0 or above receive a 50 percent tuition waiver for the following semester, and students with a CGPA between 8.5 and 8.99 receive a 25 percent waiver. To be eligible, a student must have no backlogs and an attendance of at least 85 percent. The merit scholarship is considered automatically, and no application is needed.

## Need-based scholarship

Students whose family annual income is below Rs 4,00,000 can apply for a need-based waiver of up to 75 percent of the tuition fee. An income certificate must be attached to the application. Applications must reach the Scholarship Cell by 31 August for the odd semester and by 31 January for the even semester.

## Sports scholarship

Players who have represented the country at national level receive a 30 percent tuition waiver, subject to certification by the Director of Sports.

## Renewal and withdrawal

Every scholarship is reviewed at the end of each semester. A scholarship is withdrawn if the student's CGPA falls below the required threshold or if the student is found guilty of academic misconduct.

## Combining scholarships

A student can receive only one scholarship at a time. If a student qualifies for more than one, the scheme with the higher waiver applies.
